Why Charleston Is High Risk

This City Was Built for Water Problems

Living in the Lowcountry means living with water — and that means every home is one storm, broken pipe, or humid summer away from a serious damage event.

Hurricane & Storm Flooding

Tidal flooding on the Peninsula, storm surge in James Island and Johns Island — flooding events regularly damage foundations, crawl spaces, and interiors.

Year-Round Extreme Humidity

Charleston averages 75%+ relative humidity. Mold can begin growing in as little as 24–48 hours — often before you even notice the problem.

Aging Home Stock

Many Charleston homes — especially on the Peninsula, Wagener Terrace, and Avondale — are 50–100+ years old with aging plumbing and poor vapor barriers.

Common Questions

Charleston Homeowners Ask Us

How fast does mold grow after water damage in Charleston? +
In Charleston's climate, mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure. The combination of humidity, warmth, and organic materials like wood and drywall creates ideal conditions. This is why emergency response time matters so much — the faster moisture is extracted and the area dried, the lower the chance of a mold problem developing on top of the initial water damage.
Does homeowner's insurance cover mold remediation in South Carolina? +
It depends on the cause. If mold results from a sudden and accidental event — like a burst pipe or appliance failure — it's typically covered under your standard homeowner's policy. Mold from long-term neglect is usually excluded. South Carolina homeowners with flood insurance may have separate coverage for storm-related events. We document everything to give you the best shot at a successful claim.
What are the signs of mold in a Charleston home? +
Common signs: a musty smell that doesn't go away, dark spots on walls or ceilings, worsening allergy or respiratory symptoms indoors, peeling paint, and water stains. Crawl space mold is especially common in Charleston and often goes undetected. If you haven't had your crawl space inspected recently, it's worth doing — our free inspection covers both above and below ground.
How long does water damage remediation take? +
Drying typically takes 3–5 days with commercial equipment. Mold remediation, if needed, adds 1–5 days depending on extent. Reconstruction follows after clearance testing confirms the environment is clean. Total timelines range from 1 week for minor incidents to 3–4 weeks for significant flooding.
Is crawl space mold common in Charleston, SC? +
Extremely common. Charleston sits at sea level with a high water table, and most older homes have vented crawl spaces that pull in humid air year-round — a near-perfect environment for mold on floor joists, subfloor sheathing, and insulation. Crawl space encapsulation is the long-term fix. We offer free crawl space inspections as part of any assessment visit.
What should I do immediately after flooding in my Charleston home? +
First, make sure it's safe to enter — turn off electricity at the breaker if water is near outlets. Document everything with photos and video before touching anything. Call a remediation company immediately; don't wait to see if things dry out on their own. Remove valuables if safe. Avoid using household fans — without proper equipment, this can spread mold spores. Call us and we'll walk you through next steps while dispatch is on the way.
